Who we are

Not just an NGO.
A movement.

United Beings Foundation is a youth-led Section 8 non-profit bridging rural and urban India through STEM education and skill development. By blending community heritage with cutting-edge technologies, we prepare the next generation to be job-ready, entrepreneurial, and purpose-driven.

Our learning hubs and creative initiatives provide young learners with inspiring spaces to grow, connect, and access opportunities that shape a brighter future — because opportunity should never be a privilege.

01 — Mission

Education without barriers. Opportunity without limits.

We are dedicated to making STEM education accessible to young learners from economically disadvantaged backgrounds — going beyond rural communities to reach every young person excluded from conventional education. Through inclusive, engaging, and hands-on learning experiences, we equip the next generation with essential skills to thrive in the era of the Fourth Industrial Revolution and beyond.

02 — Vision

A world where every young person gets a fair shot.

We envision a world free from bias and economic disparity, where every young person is empowered through quality education, moral grounding, and equal opportunity. By nurturing these values across the next generation, we aim to build a harmonious, inclusive, and globally connected community where no young person's potential is ever left behind.

What we do

Six programmes.
One purpose.

Equipping the next generation with the tools, spaces, and opportunities to build the India they deserve — with access ensured for those who need it most.

01
VriddhagamiTransforming spaces for the next generation
Growth · Future · Transformation
Vriddhagami
Sanskrit: Vriddha (growth) + Agami (future)

Vriddhagami transforms learning and healing environments in government schools, hospitals, and orphanages — creating vibrant, youth-centred spaces that nurture focus, creativity, and well-being. A journey of transformation where the next generation steps into a brighter future surrounded by inspiration and possibility.

  • Classrooms revitalised with creative designs that spark curiosity and focus in young learners
  • Paediatric wards brightened to provide comfort, stimulation, and healing energy for young patients
  • Orphanages supported in building nurturing, safe, and imaginative spaces for youth
  • A network of passionate artists, volunteers, and creative professionals connected to the cause
02
Art DenUnder Vriddhagami · Classes 6–9
Creative Expression · Under Vriddhagami
Art Den
15-day immersive art programme · Classes 6–9

A 15-day immersive art programme for young learners from economically challenged backgrounds. We nurture artistic talent, encourage self-expression, and open doors to future creative opportunities — because every young person deserves access to the creative economy, regardless of background.

  • Hands-on workshops in painting, drawing, and sculpture led by working artists
  • Ongoing mentorship and updates on competitions, scholarships, and events
  • Art Hubs — accessible creative spaces established in major cities for continued practice
  • Community partnerships with schools and local groups to reach youth most in need
03
Destino ArteUnder Vriddhagami · Wall Art · Remote Schools
Wall Art · Remote Outreach · Cultural Identity
Destino Arte
Vibrant wall art for remote schools & institutions

Bringing vibrant wall art to remote schools, orphanages, and institutions — transforming dull walls into inspiring canvases that spark imagination, reflect local culture, and make every young person feel that their environment belongs to them. Destino Arte reaches where others don't.

  • Creative Spaces — artistic walls that tell stories of culture, aspiration, and pride
  • Remote Outreach — bringing colour to communities that receive the least investment
  • Collaboration — artists, volunteers, and communities as co-creators of their own spaces
  • Cultural Connection — every design reflects local heritage, languages, and community identity
04
ArivioKnowledge in Action · STEAM · Rural Hubs
Knowledge in Action · STEAM · Rural Learning Hubs
Arivio
Tamil: Arivu (knowledge) — inclusive STEAM education

Rooted in the Right to Education, Arivio delivers inclusive, high-quality STEAM learning to every young learner — without bias or barriers. Through hands-on, real-world experiences in rural learning hubs, we equip the next generation with ethical values, curiosity, and future-ready skills for the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

  • Equitable STEAM — high-quality education without bias or barriers, accessible to all learners
  • Hands-on learning — real-world, experiential education in science, technology, engineering, arts & maths
  • Rural Learning Hubs — bringing STEAM resources and teaching to underserved communities
  • 4IR Skills — preparing the next generation to thrive in the modern world and beyond
05
InspiraZUBF Flagship · Campus Leaders Programme
Campus Leaders · UBF Flagship Programme
InspiraZ
Igniting leadership in today's youth changemakers

UBF's flagship Campus Leaders Programme — crafted to ignite the spark of leadership in today's youth. Partnering with academic institutions, InspiraZ designs sustainable outreach initiatives and dynamic engagement programmes that nurture creativity, collaboration, and purpose-driven community impact.

  • Leadership Development — cultivating civic responsibility and leadership skills in young people
  • Sustainable Initiatives — designing long-term outreach programmes that address real community needs
  • Innovative Problem-Solving — encouraging youth to create solutions for local and global challenges
  • Community Impact — empowering young leaders to drive meaningful, measurable change
06
BlueSweepEco-Driven Youth Initiative · Environmental Action
Eco Action · Coastal Cleanup · Waste-to-Wealth
BlueSweep
Youth-led environmental action at scale

BlueSweep is an eco-driven youth initiative inspiring the next generation to take the lead in plastic cleanup and environmental action. By mobilising young people from schools and colleges, BlueSweep protects oceans, marine life, and land animals — while unlocking waste-to-wealth innovation for youth entrepreneurs.

  • Youth Engagement — mobilising school and college communities for city and coastal cleanup drives
  • Waste to Wealth — educating young participants on turning waste into business opportunities
  • Ocean & Wildlife Safety — reducing plastic threats to marine life, land animals, and ecosystems
  • Global Partnerships — collaborating with national and international organisations for wider impact

Campus-to-Community
Campus Leaders Driving Real Community Impact

InspiraZ is not a leadership workshop or a certificate programme — it is a structured system for turning campus youth into active community changemakers. Partnering directly with academic institutions, it creates measurable, sustained community outcomes while building the next generation of civic leaders.

Need-Based Access
A Creative Economy On-Ramp for Underserved Youth

Art Den is not an after-school activity — it is a deliberate 15-day immersive entry point into the creative economy for young people from economically challenged backgrounds who have no other pathway into arts education. With need-based access ensuring no one is turned away, the combination of hands-on training, city-wide Art Hubs, and ongoing mentorship makes it genuinely one of a kind.

Art Den Creative Economy Need-Based Access
🌍
Citywide Scale
UBF Green Chennai — India's Largest Youth Plantation Drive

Targeting 1–3 lakh native saplings planted in a single day across 20–25 simultaneous sites, with a Guinness World Records attempt, GCC endorsement, and 3,000 volunteers — organised entirely by a youth-led non-profit. There is no comparable event in Tamil Nadu's history, and none organised at this scale by a Section 8 NGO in India.
